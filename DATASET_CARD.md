# Dataset card

Version: 1.0.0. Synthetic relational enterprise data, January–June 2026 UTC. See data/manifest.json for exact counts and checksums. Formats: CSV, SQLite, JSON, JSONL.

Intended uses: education, EDA, SQL, software engineering, AI and workload simulation. Historical human routing is noisy, not ground truth. Template-based text and simulated operations limit real-world generalization. Missingness and operational errors can be intentional. No real customer or employee records are intentionally included. Do not infer real-world business or demographic outcomes. Separate features known at ingestion from outcomes observed later. Public records are not a private hiring benchmark.
