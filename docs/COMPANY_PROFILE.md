# Company profile

Northstar is a fictional B2B SaaS enterprise supplying business productivity and workflow automation software to individuals and organizations through subscriptions. Its service desk handles Billing Operations, Account Services and Technical Support, with a Needs Review queue for ambiguous requests. Customers generate invoices, payments, refunds, messages and feedback. Engineering teams release products and respond to incidents; operations teams manage staffing, assignments and SLA performance.

The first release depicts six months of simulated operations before planned digital transformation. Future releases may extend the timeline as new business problems and community projects emerge. The product catalogue in data/products.csv is the authoritative catalogue for this release.
